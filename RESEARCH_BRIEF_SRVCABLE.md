# SRVCABLE (Sarvottam Cables) – One-Page Research Brief

## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. In its Q1 FY27 consolidated company release, revenue from operations was ₹1,248 crore, up 18% YoY; EBITDA was ₹140 crore with an 11.2% margin, down 170 bps; and profit after tax was ₹82 crore, up 5.9%. A news report cited Q1 revenue of ₹1,428 crore, up 35%. Order book stood at ₹3,900 crore versus ₹3,150 crore a year earlier, and exports contributed 21% of revenue versus 16%. The new Bharuch plant, adding about 30% to cable capacity, is on track for commercial production in Q3 FY27. Management maintains FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17%. Shares were at ₹1,012 on NSE after Q1 results, down 3.2%. Promoter holding was 58.2% as of June 2026, with FII at 11.8% and DII at 14.6%.

## 2. Bull Case
- **Order book visibility:** Order book rose to ₹3,900 crore from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier, supporting near-term demand visibility.
- **Capacity expansion:** The Bharuch plant adds about 30% capacity, with ₹620 crore capex, about 70% spent, funded mainly through internal accruals; net debt to equity is 0.35 times.
- **Data-centre demand:** Data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue, and management expects this to double in two years, supported by strong power transmission and data-centre demand.
- **Export mix improvement:** Exports increased to 21% of revenue from 16% a year earlier, indicating improving geographic diversification.

## 3. Bear Case
- **Copper cost pressure:** Copper prices rose nearly 14% during Q1, compressing EBITDA margin by 170 bps; price-variation clauses reset with a lag of one to two quarters.
- **Receivables stress:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, mainly due to slower payments from two large state utility customers.
- **GST demand exposure:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, relating to FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 input tax credit claims.
- **Governance/pledge concern:** A 2024 report said promoter pledge rose to 35%, while the June 2026 filing shows 4.1% of promoter holding pledged, leaving the current pledge status needing confirmation.

## 4. Open Questions
- Reconcile Q1 FY27 revenue: the company release reports ₹1,248 crore, while Business Daily reports ₹1,428 crore.
- Confirm whether the Nagpur penalty on “Sarvottam Cable Network” relates to the listed company and whether it has any material compliance or financial impact.
- Clarify promoter pledge history and current status, given the 2024 report of 35% pledge and the June 2026 filing showing 4.1%.
- Monitor copper cost pass-through, state-utility receivable collections, and the outcome of the ₹46.3 crore GST appeal.
